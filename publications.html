Urban Reindustrialization
Park, Jeong-Il (2026). In Richardson Dilworth (ed.), Oxford Bibliographies in Urban Studies. New York, NY: Oxford Academic. Published 15 September 2026.
doi.org/10.1093/9780197859209.003.0112 →
This peer-reviewed reference entry, published in Oxford Bibliographies in Urban Studies (Oxford University Press), provides a comprehensive scholarly guide to urban reindustrialization — the resurgence of manufacturing within deindustrialized, service-oriented cities. The entry traces how production was progressively displaced from urban cores by environmental regulations, restrictive zoning, and the spatial demands of mass production, and examines how digitalization and Industry 4.0 technologies are now reversing this trend by enabling clean, space-efficient manufacturing even within dense urban environments. Organized across eight thematic sections — from general overviews and empirical cases to planning frameworks, spatial design typologies, and future research directions — it annotates the most significant peer-reviewed studies, books, and policy reports in the field. Researchers, graduate students, urban planners, and policymakers will find it an efficient and authoritative entry point for literature reviews, research agenda-setting, and navigating the growing body of scholarship on urban manufacturing and industrial policy.
