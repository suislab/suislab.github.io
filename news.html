SUIS Lab officially launched at Gachon University
Prof. Jeong-Il Park joins Gachon University (가천대학교) as Associate Professor in the Department of Urban Planning, establishing the SUIS (Sustainable Urban Industrial Space) Lab. The lab focuses on urban industrial land dynamics, digitalization of production, and sustainable regional economic development.

NRF research project (737M KRW) successfully concluded
The 4-year National Research Foundation project "An empirical analysis on the mechanism of digitalization of production and industrial reurbanization" has been completed. Key findings have been published in multiple SSCI journals including Cities, Technology in Society, and Asian Journal of Technology Innovation.
